⚔ HOW THE FIGHT WORKS

⛏ CLIMBYour basic attack. Also earns 1 momentum.
🛡 BRACECuts the boss's next hit to 35%. Earns 1 momentum. Use it when the boss telegraphs a HEAVY or DEVASTATING move.
🍫 RATIONHeals 33% of your max HP. Only 3 per fight.
⚡ SUMMIT PUSHSpends all 3 momentum for roughly 3x damage and a high crit chance.
The boss announces its next move before you choose — that's your cue. Light hits you can tank. Heavy and Devastating hits you should BRACE. A clean loop is: CLIMB → CLIMB → BRACE → SUMMIT PUSH, healing whenever you drop under half. Your damage and HP scale with your rank, so clearing day hikes literally makes you stronger. Losing costs you nothing.
