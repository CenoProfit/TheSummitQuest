### front.w1 · Alberta Falls
A 30 ft drop on Glacier Creek and the busiest short trail in the park. Go at seven or not at all.

### front.w2 · Ouzel Falls
Up Wild Basin past Copeland and Calypso to a 40 ft fall, through forest that burned in 1978 and is still growing back.

### front.w3 · Adams Falls & Cascade
On the quiet west side above Grand Lake, where the water drops into a slot you hear before you see.

### front.w4 · Boulder Falls
Seventy feet, five minutes from the car, straight up Boulder Canyon. Closed for years after rockfall; check before you drive.

### front.w5 · Bridal Veil Falls, Cow Creek
The quiet north end of the park, up a meadow drainage to a 20 ft fall in a rock alcove. Elk are usually in the first mile.

### front.s1 · Royal Arch, Chautauqua
Up under the Flatirons to a sandstone arch, 1,400 ft of climbing that Boulder treats as a lunch break.

### front.s2 · Bear Lake Loop
Flat around a lake at 9,450 ft with Hallett Peak standing at the far end. The gateway to the whole park.

### front.s3 · Mount Sanitas
1,300 ft straight up the edge of town on rock steps. Boulder's fitness test, and everyone here is fitter than you.

### front.s4 · Dream & Emerald Lakes
Three lakes stepping up under Hallett and Flattop. Wind funnels down the cirque and it can be brutal.

### front.s5 · Lily Mountain
A small summit just outside the park boundary, so no reservation and no shuttle, with a scramble to finish.

### front.v1 · Trail Ridge Road: Alpine Visitor Center
The highest paved through-road in the country, topping 12,183 ft. Eleven miles of it are above treeline.

### front.v2 · Mount Blue Sky Summit
A 14,130 ft summit you can nearly drive to, with bristlecone pines below that were old before Rome. Renamed from Evans in 2023.

### front.v3 · Lookout Mountain
Buffalo Bill's grave and the whole Denver metro laid out on the plain below. Fifteen minutes from Golden.

### front.v4 · Loveland Pass
Park on the Divide at 11,990 ft and walk the ridge from there. Altitude arrives immediately at this elevation.

### front.m1 · Chasm Lake
To the foot of the Diamond, the 1,000 ft vertical face of Longs Peak. Most people go no further, and it is the better hike.

### front.m2 · Sky Pond
Past Loch Vale and up a waterfall you climb through, hands on rock, to a lake under Taylor Peak.

### front.m3 · Lake Isabelle
Indian Peaks at their most photogenic: a shallow lake with Navajo and Apache standing over it. Parking fills by six.

### front.m4 · Herman Gulch
Straight off I-70 into a wildflower basin at 12,000 ft. Best July meadow in the state for the least driving.

### front.m5 · Saint Mary's Glacier
A permanent snowfield above a lake at 10,700 ft. Not a glacier, but people ski it in July regardless.

### front.h1 · Longs Peak: Keyhole Route
5,000 ft of climbing and then the Ledges, the Trough, the Narrows and the Homestretch, all of it exposed. Leave the trailhead by three in the morning.

### front.h2 · Grays & Torreys
Two fourteeners on one ridge, the easiest pair in the state, and still 3,600 ft of climbing above 11,000.

### front.h3 · Quandary Peak
The gentlest fourteener in the Ten Mile range, which still means 3,450 ft and mountain goats that want your salt.

### front.h4 · Mount Bierstadt
Across a willow bog and up a broad ridge. The Sawtooth traverse to Blue Sky from here is a different order of thing entirely.

### front.h5 · James Peak
A 13,294 ft summit on the Divide above St Mary's, with cirques on the north side holding snow year round.

### front.l1 · Flattop Mountain to Hallett
Up onto the tundra shelf and along the Divide. Two miles of it are fully exposed, and the weather turns at noon.

### front.l2 · Pawnee Pass
Over the Divide at 12,550 ft past Lake Isabelle, on a trail cut into a shelf above the cirque.

### front.l3 · Chicago Lakes
Two lakes in a granite basin under the north face of Blue Sky, through burn from the 1978 fire.

### front.l4 · Devils Thumb Pass
Up to the Divide at 11,800 ft and a rock pillar standing on the crest. Wind here is constant and personal.

### front.x1 · Four Passes: Indian Peaks Traverse
Pawnee, Buchanan, Arapaho and Devils Thumb in one push along the Divide. Above treeline for most of it, with nowhere to shelter.

### front.x2 · Wild Basin to Thunder Lake
The quiet southeast corner of the park, up past three waterfalls to a lake at 10,600 ft with a patrol cabin on the shore.

### front.x3 · Continental Divide: Grand Lake to Bear Lake
Across the park west to east over Flattop, from the headwaters of the Colorado to the Bear Lake road.

### front.x4 · Gore Range Traverse
No maintained trail for long stretches, no fourteeners to draw crowds, and some of the roughest country in the state.

### front.x5 · Never Summer Range Loop
Over Thunder Pass past Lake Agnes on the far west boundary. The range runs north to south, which no other range here does.

### front.x6 · Longs Peak: Loft & Keplinger's
The route Longs was first climbed by in 1868, camping high and going over the Loft rather than through the Keyhole.

### front.x7 · Lost Creek Wilderness Loop
Granite domes and a creek that disappears underground and comes back nine times. Nothing else in Colorado looks like it.
