### glacier.w1 · Avalanche Lake
Up a gorge the creek has cut into red rock, to a lake with four waterfalls dropping into the far end off Bearhat.

### glacier.w2 · Running Eagle Falls
Called Trick Falls because in high water it pours over the top and in low water it comes straight out of the cliff face instead.

### glacier.w3 · St Mary & Virginia Falls
Two falls off the Sun Road on the dry east side. Virginia is 50 ft and you can walk into the spray at the base.

### glacier.w4 · Holland Falls
Along the north shore of Holland Lake in the Swan, with the Bob Marshall wall standing behind it.

### glacier.w5 · Grinnell Lake
Rock flour off the glacier above turns the water a flat opaque turquoise. Two boat rides cut the walk in half if you would rather.

### glacier.s1 · Trail of the Cedars
Boardwalk under 500 year old western red cedars, in the only inland temperate rainforest this far north.

### glacier.s2 · Hidden Lake Overlook
Boardwalk up from Logan Pass through the meadow to a shelf above the lake. Mountain goats treat the handrail as furniture.

### glacier.s3 · Apgar Lookout
Three long switchbacks to a fire lookout looking straight up Lake McDonald into the heart of the park.

### glacier.s4 · Danny On Trail, Whitefish Mountain
Up the ski hill in summer, with the chairlift running if your legs give out. Flathead Lake and the whole Whitefish Range from the top.

### glacier.s5 · Rocky Point & Beaver Lake
Through the 2003 burn to a rock point on Lake McDonald. The dead trees make the view better, not worse.

### glacier.v1 · Logan Pass, Going-to-the-Sun Road
6,646 ft on the Divide, on a road blasted into the Garden Wall in the 1920s. Vehicle reservation required most of the summer.

### glacier.v2 · Scenic Point, Two Medicine
2,300 ft up onto a bare shoulder where the mountains end and the plains run east with nothing in the way of them.

### glacier.v3 · Mount Aeneas, Jewel Basin
A 7,528 ft summit with Flathead Lake on one side and 27 lakes of the Jewel Basin on the other. Hiking only, no stock, no bikes.

### glacier.v4 · Numa Ridge Lookout
The North Fork, reached by a washboard gravel road and a 2,900 ft climb. Almost nobody comes, which is the point.

### glacier.m1 · Iceberg Lake
A cirque so deep in shadow that ice floats on the lake into August, under a 3,000 ft headwall.

### glacier.m2 · Grinnell Glacier
1,600 ft up a ledge above two lakes to what is left of the glacier. It has lost most of its area since the park was named.

### glacier.m3 · Cracker Lake
The most saturated water in the park, under the 4,000 ft north face of Siyeh, with an abandoned mine and a rusting boiler at the shore.

### glacier.m4 · Ptarmigan Tunnel
A 240 ft tunnel blasted straight through the ridge in 1930. You walk in on one side of the Divide and out on the other.

### glacier.m5 · Highline to Haystack Butte
A ledge cut into the Garden Wall with a hand cable bolted to the rock for the first quarter mile and a long drop below it.

### glacier.h1 · Siyeh Pass
2,240 ft to an 8,080 ft pass, then down the other side to the Sun Road. The best single day in the park if you can shuttle it.

### glacier.h2 · Mount Siyeh
10,014 ft with no maintained trail above the pass, scree and route finding the whole way, and one of the tallest cliffs in North America on the far side.

### glacier.h3 · Swiftcurrent Pass & Lookout
Past four lakes, up a wall of switchbacks to the Divide, then higher again to a lookout at 8,436 ft.

### glacier.h4 · Piegan Pass
Up through Preston Park, some of the best larch and meadow in the park, to a pass at 7,570 ft between Piegan and Cataract.

### glacier.h5 · Mount Brown Lookout
4,300 ft of climbing in five miles on relentless switchbacks. The hardest ordinary day hike in the park and it never lets up.

### glacier.l1 · Highline Trail to The Loop
Logan Pass to the Sun Road switchback, along the Garden Wall the whole way with a side trip to Granite Park Chalet. Ride the shuttle back.

### glacier.l2 · Gunsight Pass
Jackson Glacier to Lake McDonald over a pass at 6,946 ft, past three lakes and a stone shelter on the crest. One way, shuttle back.

### glacier.l3 · Dawson & Pitamakan Loop
Two passes and four miles of open ridge in between, with Rising Wolf on one side the entire time. Exposed to weather with no way off.

### glacier.l4 · Sperry Chalet & Comeau Pass
To a stone chalet built in 1913, then up a staircase carved into the rock with a fixed cable to reach the pass above it.

### glacier.x1 · Chief Mountain to Many Glacier
The northeast corner past Elizabeth and Cosley Lakes under Mount Merritt, with Chief Mountain standing out on the plains behind you.

### glacier.x2 · The Chinese Wall, Bob Marshall
Twelve miles of unbroken limestone escarpment standing 1,000 ft above the meadow, in a wilderness the size of Delaware with no roads in it.

### glacier.x3 · North Circle Loop
Many Glacier to Fifty Mountain to Goat Haunt and back over Swiftcurrent. Backcountry permit, bear canister, and a real plan.

### glacier.x4 · Mission Mountains: Glacier Lake
Steep, wet and unmarked in places, in a wilderness that closes part of itself every summer so the grizzlies can feed undisturbed.

### glacier.x5 · Nyack & Coal Creek Loop
The park's designated wild area: no maintained tread, no bridges, and a ford of the Middle Fork to start and finish. Camp where you like.

### glacier.x6 · Two Medicine to Marias Pass
The southern tip of the park along the Divide, over Firebrand Pass, on the stretch of the CDT almost nobody walks.

### glacier.x7 · Swan Crest: Alpine Trail No. 7
The whole spine of the Swan Range above Jewel Basin, dry camping most nights because the water is all a thousand feet below you.
