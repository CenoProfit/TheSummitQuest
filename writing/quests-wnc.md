### wnc.w1 · Hooker Falls
Wide swimming hole right below the falls. The easiest waterfall to reach in DuPont — flat, wide trail the whole way.

### wnc.w2 · Triple Falls
Three-tiered cascade with flat rock ledges. Wade the pools above the top tier. Steep stair climb back up.

### wnc.w3 · Bridal Veil Falls
Walk right up onto the sloping rock slab beside the falling water. Classic cliff-jump and slide spot — scout depth first.

### wnc.w4 · Sliding Rock
A 60-ft natural granite water slide into a deep plunge pool. Lifeguards in summer. The water is genuinely freezing.

### wnc.w5 · Skinny Dip Falls
Secluded multi-tier swimming hole just off the Parkway. Small jump ledges and a deep green basin.

### wnc.w6 · Midnight Hole
Deep emerald pool framed by giant boulders on Big Creek. One of the best swim and jump holes in the Smokies.

### wnc.w7 · Rainbow Falls
150-ft thundering drop in Gorges State Park. Mist throws real rainbows across the gorge on sunny afternoons.

### wnc.w8 · Turtleback Falls
Natural rock waterslide upstream of Rainbow Falls. Popular but genuinely dangerous — the current runs toward the next drop.

### wnc.w9 · Whitewater Falls
411 ft — the tallest waterfall east of the Rockies. Paved overlook walk, then optional stairs toward the base.

### wnc.w10 · Elk River Falls
Short walk to a broad 50-ft plunge near Banner Elk. Well-known local jump spot — strong swimmers only.

### wnc.w11 · Little Bradley Falls
Creek crossings and boulder scrambling lead to a tall ribbon falls with a deep pool. Expect wet feet.

### wnc.w12 · Catawba Falls
Rebuilt, family-friendly grade to a mossy multi-tiered cascade near Old Fort. New staircase to the upper deck.

### wnc.w13 · Second Falls
Wide falls at the bottom of the Graveyard Fields basin. Wading pools below, blueberry bushes everywhere in late summer.

### wnc.w14 · Tallulah Gorge
Suspension bridge strung across a 1,000-ft gorge, plus a permit-only gorge-floor route with sliding rock and swim holes.

### wnc.w15 · Bust Your Butt Falls
A 20-foot multi-tier cascade on the Cullasaja with a deep pool under it and a natural rock slide alongside. Officially Quarry Falls; nobody calls it that. Check the depth before you jump, and skip the slide entirely when the river is running high.

### wnc.w16 · Blue Hole Falls
A 25-foot drop into a deep turquoise pool on High Shoals Creek, with the 100-foot High Shoals Falls further down the same trail. Gravel forest road and a creek ford to reach the trailhead, then 540 feet of climbing on the way back out.

### wnc.w17 · Paradise Falls
Wolf Creek drops 100 ft into a slot canyon with an emerald pool at the bottom. Short but genuinely technical — steep, loose, and rope-assisted in places. The Forest Service advises against the route to the top, and there is a dam upstream that can raise the water without warning.

### wnc.s1 · Jump Off Rock
Best sunset within twenty minutes of home. Park at the top and walk out to a wide-open Blue Ridge panorama.

### wnc.s2 · Moore Cove Falls
Gentle grade to a 50-ft veil you can walk completely behind. A great cold-weather hike — the ice formations get wild.

### wnc.s3 · Looking Glass Falls
Visible straight from the parking pullout. Stairs drop you to the base pool for a quick, freezing dip.

### wnc.s4 · Devil's Courthouse
Steep but paved half mile to a bare rock summit. Four states visible on a clear day. Peregrine falcons nest here.

### wnc.s5 · Craggy Pinnacle
A tunnel of gnarled rhododendron opens onto a 360° bald. Unreal in mid-June bloom and at sunset.

### wnc.s6 · Rough Ridge
Boardwalk over fragile heath opens to the money shot of the Linn Cove Viaduct curling around Grandfather.

### wnc.s7 · Beacon Heights
Short walk ending in a slabby rock scramble with wide-open views toward Grandfather and Table Rock.

### wnc.s8 · Rumbling Bald Sunset Rock
Free trail with no park ticket needed. Broad view down Hickory Nut Gorge over Lake Lure.

### wnc.v1 · Waterrock Knob
One of the highest points on the Parkway. Layered Smokies ridgelines stack to the horizon — elite sunrise spot.

### wnc.v2 · Wiseman's View
The definitive look into Linville Gorge — Hawksbill and Table Rock rise straight across the canyon.

### wnc.v3 · Chimney Rock State Park
Elevator and stairs to a 315-ft granite monolith with a 75-mile view. Hickory Nut Falls trail included.

### wnc.v4 · Big Glassy Mountain
Walk past Carl Sandburg's goats at Connemara and finish on an open granite dome. Absurdly close to home.

### wnc.v5 · Linn Cove Viaduct
The engineering marvel of the Parkway, hugging Grandfather Mountain on stilts. Walk beneath it from the visitor center.

### wnc.v6 · Green Knob Overlook
Quiet, rarely-crowded Parkway pullout below Mount Mitchell with big valley depth. Great cloud-inversion spot.

### wnc.m1 · Triple + High Falls Loop
The signature DuPont outing — three major waterfalls and the covered bridge in one loop. Sunrise start beats the parking crush.

### wnc.m2 · Black Balsam Knob Loop
Open grassy balds above treeline. Feels like the Scottish Highlands dropped into North Carolina. Bring a windbreaker.

### wnc.m3 · Sam Knob
Twin grassy summits with a meadow saddle between them. Same views as Black Balsam next door, a fraction of the people.

### wnc.m4 · Max Patch Loop
The most photographed bald on the Appalachian Trail. Full 360° with the Smokies laid out west. Camping now prohibited on top.

### wnc.m5 · Catawba Falls Upper
Push past the lower falls on the newer staircase route to reach the hidden upper cascade. Steep and worth it.

### wnc.m6 · Green River Cove Trail
Follows a whitewater gorge with a boulder field at the halfway point built for a picnic. Check post-Helene conditions.

### wnc.m7 · John Rock
Granite dome staring straight at Looking Glass Rock across the valley. Loop it past Cedar Rock for more open slab.

### wnc.m8 · Table Rock (SC)
Sustained climb to a broad granite summit above Table Rock Reservoir. Best upstate South Carolina hike, period.

### wnc.h1 · Looking Glass Rock
Relentless switchbacks up 1,500 ft to a bare granite summit. Mica in the trail dirt actually sparkles. Elite payoff.

### wnc.h2 · Mount Pisgah
Short and steep to a summit fire tower with a 180° sweep. Rocky and rooty near the top — expect to use your hands.

### wnc.h3 · Chimney Tops
Steepest maintained trail in the Smokies. Ends at an observation deck — the final pinnacle has been closed since the 2016 fire.

### wnc.h4 · Shortoff Mountain
Remote southern rim of Linville Gorge with long open-rock ridge walking and almost no crowds. Zero shade — carry water.

### wnc.h5 · Hawksbill Mountain
Brutally steep for its length, then a jagged summit hanging over the gorge. Best effort-to-view ratio in the East.

### wnc.h6 · Grandfather Trail
Ladders bolted to rock, cables, and full-body scrambles along the ridge from the Swinging Bridge over MacRae Peak. The most technical day hike here.

### wnc.h7 · Art Loeb: Balsam to Ivestor
Ridge-walking section through Shining Rock Wilderness. No blazes in wilderness — bring a map and know your bailouts.

### wnc.l1 · Mount Mitchell Crest Trail
Ridge traverse to 6,684 ft — the highest point east of the Mississippi. Brutal, rooty, and spectacular.

### wnc.l2 · Alum Cave to Mt LeConte
Through Arch Rock and under the Alum Cave Bluffs to the third-highest peak in the Smokies. Cable handholds on the ledges.

### wnc.l3 · Charlies Bunion
The classic AT day hike from Newfound Gap to an exposed rock outcrop with sheer drops on both sides.

### wnc.l4 · Panthertown Valley Loop
The 'Yosemite of the East' — granite domes, waterfalls, and a flat sandy valley floor. Navigation is genuinely tricky.

### wnc.l5 · Linville Gorge Rim-to-Rim
Table Rock and Hawksbill linked in one big day along the eastern rim. Rugged, exposed, and unforgiving in heat.

### wnc.l6 · Shining Rock Wilderness Loop
White quartz outcrops that give the wilderness its name, linked through balds and old burn scars. No signage by design.

### wnc.l7 · Roan: Carvers to Grassy Ridge
Miles of unbroken grassy bald along the AT. Arguably the best sustained ridge views in the entire Appalachians.

### wnc.l8 · Profile Trail
Roughly 2,000 ft of steady switchbacks up the west flank of Grandfather from NC 105, ending at Calloway Gap where the Grandfather Trail takes over. Turn around there, or carry on if you have the daylight and the nerve.

### wnc.x1 · Art Loeb Trail (Thru)
NC's classic point-to-point: Davidson River up through Pilot Mountain, Black Balsam, and Shining Rock to Daniel Boone. Shuttle required.

### wnc.x2 · Foothills Trail
The SC/NC escarpment traverse through Jocassee Gorges. Waterfalls nearly every single day. Very doable in sections.

### wnc.x3 · Mount LeConte Overnight
Sleep near the summit at LeConte Lodge or the shelter and catch sunrise from Myrtle Point. The lodge books a year out.

### wnc.x4 · Panthertown Backpack Loop
The easiest entry into overnight backpacking here — established sites near waterfalls, flat valley floor, water everywhere.

### wnc.x5 · Linville Gorge Loop
Rim-and-river loop through the 'Grand Canyon of the East.' Permits required on weekends May–Oct. Navigation is serious.

### wnc.x6 · Roan Highlands Traverse
Carvers Gap to Hughes Gap along the AT with shelters spaced out. Bald-to-bald ridge camping with zero tree cover.

### wnc.x7 · Shining Rock Backpack Loop
Art Loeb sections stitched with connectors for a full wilderness immersion. Water sources are scarce on the ridge.
