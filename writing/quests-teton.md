### teton.w1 · Hidden Falls & Inspiration Point
Boat across Jenny Lake and climb to a 100 ft fall, then up to a ledge over the whole of Jackson Hole. Walk around the lake instead and it is 5 miles.

### teton.w2 · Lower Yellowstone Falls Brink
Down 600 ft of switchbacks to stand at the lip where the river drops 308 ft into the canyon. The climb back out is the hard part.

### teton.w3 · Fairy Falls
Flat walk past the overlook above Grand Prismatic to a 200 ft ribbon fall in old burn forest.

### teton.w4 · Mystic Falls
Up the Little Firehole to a 70 ft fall, then a spur climbing to an overlook of the whole Upper Geyser Basin.

### teton.w5 · String & Leigh Lakes
Flat and sandy between two lakes with the Tetons standing straight up out of the far shore. Best swimming in the park.

### teton.s1 · Taggart Lake
Through a 1985 burn now grown back in aspen, to a moraine lake right under the Grand. First hike of the season most years.

### teton.s2 · Schwabacher Landing
Beaver ponds on the Snake that mirror the whole range at dawn. The photograph everyone takes, and it is early enough that you will be alone.

### teton.s3 · Grand Prismatic Overlook
The only place you see the spring as a whole rather than as steam. From the boardwalk below it is just fog.

### teton.s4 · Mount Washburn
An old road to a fire lookout at 10,243 ft, with bighorn on the slopes and the whole caldera laid out below.

### teton.s5 · Signal Mountain Summit
A lone hill in the middle of the valley, so it looks at the range side-on rather than head-on. Drive it if you must.

### teton.v1 · Snake River Overlook
Ansel Adams stood here in 1942. The cottonwoods have grown since, but the range has not moved.

### teton.v2 · Artist Point
The canyon view: yellow rhyolite walls and the lower falls a mile upstream. The rock really is that colour.

### teton.v3 · Oxbow Bend
A slack bend of the Snake under Mount Moran. Otters, moose and every serious lens in the valley at first light.

### teton.v4 · Lamar Valley
The best wolf watching in the lower forty-eight. Bring a spotting scope and get there before the light does.

### teton.m1 · Delta Lake
Off-trail boulder scrambling off the Amphitheatre Lake route to a glacial lake the colour of antifreeze, right under the Grand.

### teton.m2 · Amphitheater Lake
Switchbacks gaining 3,000 ft to two lakes in a cirque at the base of the Grand's east face.

### teton.m3 · Phelps Lake Loop
Around the lake from the Rockefeller preserve, past a jumping rock that everyone claims they will use.

### teton.m4 · Titcomb Basin Approach
Up the Elkhart Park trail into the Wind Rivers, granite everywhere and far fewer people than the Tetons.

### teton.m5 · Bunsen Peak
An old volcanic plug above Mammoth, 1,300 ft up for the Gallatin range and the Yellowstone River valley.

### teton.h1 · Static Peak Divide
The highest maintained trail in the park at 10,790 ft. Switchbacks that do not stop, and snow on the divide into July.

### teton.h2 · Table Mountain
The west side approach, and the view William Henry Jackson photographed in 1872. The Grand fills the entire sky.

### teton.h3 · Paintbrush Divide
A 10,700 ft notch that holds snow most of the summer. An ice axe is not optional before August, whatever the trailhead register says.

### teton.h4 · Avalanche Canyon to Lake Taminah
Unmaintained route up a canyon most people walk past, ending under Shoshoko Falls and the Wall.

### teton.l1 · Cascade Canyon to Lake Solitude
Up the great glacial trough between the Grand and Mount Owen to a lake in a cirque at 9,035 ft. Moose in the willows most mornings.

### teton.l2 · Death Canyon Shelf
A limestone bench running for miles at 9,500 ft with the whole range on one side and nothing on the other.

### teton.l3 · Shoshone Lake via DeLacy Creek
The largest backcountry lake in the lower forty-eight with no road to it, and a geyser basin on the far shore.

### teton.l4 · Sky Rim Trail, Gallatin
A knife ridge on the park's northwest boundary, exposed the whole way, with grizzly country on both sides.

### teton.x1 · Teton Crest Trail
Rendezvous Mountain to String Lake along the spine of the range, over Death Canyon Shelf and Paintbrush Divide. The best multi-day in the country by a margin.

### teton.x2 · Paintbrush–Cascade Loop
Over the divide at 10,700 ft and back down the great canyon. Snow on the north side into August, and a permit lottery to match.

### teton.x3 · Titcomb Basin
Into the heart of the Wind Rivers under Gannett Peak, the highest in Wyoming. Granite walls on every side and no crowds at all.

### teton.x4 · Yellowstone: Bechler River
The Cascade Corner, and the wettest quarter of the park. A dozen waterfalls, a hot spring to soak in, and river fords that decide the schedule.

### teton.x5 · Thorofare
The furthest point from a road anywhere in the lower forty-eight. Grizzly density here is the highest in the ecosystem.

### teton.x6 · Black Canyon of the Yellowstone
Downriver from Gardiner through a dry basalt canyon, with a suspension bridge and the best early-season backpacking in the park.

### teton.x7 · Granite Canyon Loop
Up one canyon and down the next with a night on the shelf between them. Bears here work the berry slopes in August.
