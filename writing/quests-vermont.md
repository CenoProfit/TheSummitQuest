### vermont.w1 · Moss Glen Falls, Stowe
A short muddy path to an 125 ft cascade in a mossy gorge. The trail is slick even when it has not rained.

### vermont.w2 · Bingham Falls
Steep steps down to a series of pools and chutes in Smugglers Notch. The bottom pool is a genuine swimming hole.

### vermont.w3 · Warren Falls
Ledges, chutes and jumping rocks on the Mad River. Busy on any hot Saturday, and worth it anyway.

### vermont.w4 · Texas Falls
A narrow gorge the water has drilled straight through, with a footbridge right over the throat of it.

### vermont.w5 · Hamilton Falls
125 ft down a granite staircase near Jamaica State Park. People have died sliding here; stay out of the upper chute.

### vermont.w6 · Arethusa Falls
The tallest single drop in New Hampshire at around 140 ft. Steady climb through hemlock, and the base is a natural amphitheatre.

### vermont.s1 · Mount Philo
Vermont's oldest state park, and the cheapest sweep of Champlain and the Adirondacks you will ever earn.

### vermont.s2 · Snake Mountain
An old carriage road to a concrete slab where a hotel once stood, looking straight down the Champlain valley.

### vermont.s3 · Stowe Pinnacle
Short, steep, and it pays out completely: Mansfield and the Worcester range across the whole valley.

### vermont.s4 · Owls Head, Groton
A CCC stone shelter on a bald knob above Kettle Pond. Ten minutes of effort for a view that does not deserve to be that cheap.

### vermont.s5 · Wright's Mountain
Quiet town forest above Bradford with a ledge looking east into the Connecticut valley and the Whites beyond.

### vermont.v1 · Smugglers Notch Overlook
A road so tight between boulders that trucks get wedged in it every summer. Closed all winter.

### vermont.v2 · Appalachian Gap
Park at the height of land and take the Long Trail north a few minutes to a ledge over the Mad River valley.

### vermont.v3 · Quechee Gorge
165 ft down to the Ottauquechee, which is deep enough that Vermont calls it a canyon and means it.

### vermont.v4 · Cannon Mountain Rim
Franconia Notch from the top of the tram, where the Old Man's profile used to be until it came off in 2003.

### vermont.m1 · Camel's Hump via Burrows
The undeveloped one: no road, no tower, no summit building. Bare alpine tundra on top and a 360 degree view of the state.

### vermont.m2 · Mount Hunger
A steep grind to open ledges facing Mansfield and Camel's Hump at once. The last quarter mile is bare rock.

### vermont.m3 · Mount Abraham
One of only three true alpine summits in Vermont. Stay on the rock, the plants up there are older than the trail.

### vermont.m4 · Killington Peak
Second highest in the state, up the Bucklin Trail through hardwood then spruce to a rock summit above the ski lifts.

### vermont.m5 · Mount Pisgah, Willoughby
Cliffs falling straight into Lake Willoughby, which is a fjord in everything but name. Peregrines nest on the face.

### vermont.h1 · Mount Mansfield via Sunset Ridge
The highest point in Vermont, up an open ridge with exposure the whole way. Two hundred acres of arctic tundra on the summit ridge.

### vermont.h2 · Mount Washington via Tuckerman
The worst weather in America, measured and documented. 4,250 ft of climbing through a glacial cirque. People die here in every month of the year.

### vermont.h3 · Franconia Ridge Loop
Two miles above treeline linking Lafayette, Lincoln and Little Haystack. The finest ridge walk in the east, and fully exposed to whatever the sky is doing.

### vermont.h4 · Mount Hight & Carter Dome
Across the notch from the Presidentials, so you get to look at them instead of standing in the wind on them.

### vermont.h5 · Camel's Hump via Monroe
The longer eastern approach past the wreck of a B-24 that went into the mountain in 1944. Most of it is still there.

### vermont.l1 · Mount Mansfield Ridge Traverse
The whole profile end to end, Forehead to Chin, along the spine of the state. The Cliff Trail section is not for everyone.

### vermont.l2 · Mount Moosilauke Carriage Road
The southernmost of the White Mountain 4,000 footers and the gentlest way onto a big bald summit.

### vermont.l3 · Mount Ellen & the Monroe Skyline
Long Trail ridge running between Appalachian Gap and Lincoln Gap over four summits. Mud season here lasts into June.

### vermont.l4 · Jay Peak from Route 242
The last big summit before Canada, close enough that you can see the border cut through the trees.

### vermont.x1 · Long Trail: Mansfield to Smugglers
The oldest long-distance trail in the country, on its hardest northern stretch. Ladders, bog bridges and shelters that fill by four in the afternoon.

### vermont.x2 · Presidential Traverse
Every Presidential summit in one push, nineteen miles of it above treeline. There is nowhere to bail out once you are on the ridge.

### vermont.x3 · Pemigewasset Loop
A horseshoe around an entire drainage, over eight 4,000 footers, on old logging railroad grades gone back to forest.

### vermont.x4 · Long Trail: Camel's Hump Section
Over the Hump and north along the spine, with the Montclair Glen and Bamforth Ridge shelters in between.

### vermont.x5 · Willoughby State Forest Circuit
Pisgah, Hor and Bartlett around the lake in the Northeast Kingdom. Emptier than anywhere else in the state.

### vermont.x6 · Zealand to Crawford Notch
Hut to hut across the Zealand and Crawford country, past Thoreau Falls and Zeacliff, on some of the gentlest grades in the Whites.

### vermont.x7 · Long Trail: Jay to the Border
The final stretch north to Journey's End and the Canadian line. Wet, wild, and almost nobody up there.
