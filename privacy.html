★ SUMMIT — PRIVACY ★

Last updated 6 October 2026
Summit does not collect anything.
There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers belonging to this app. Nothing you do in Summit is transmitted anywhere. Everything the game knows about you stays on your phone.

Your location

Summit asks for your location only at the moment you tap to log a hike or claim a boss. It compares that reading against the coordinates of the destination to check that you are actually there, and then discards it. Your location is never stored, never written to your save, and never sent off the device. The app does not track you in the background and cannot see where you are at any other time.

Your photos

If you attach a photo to a hike, it is stored on your device and nowhere else. Photos are re-encoded before they are saved, which removes the embedded camera data, including the GPS coordinates most phones write into an image file.

There is a setting that also saves a copy to your own photo library. It is on by default and can be switched off in the options menu. That copy goes to your library on your device, under your control, and is backed up only if you back your library up yourself.

Your progress

Cleared hikes, badges, XP and your climber are stored on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Other services

Summit makes no network requests of its own. The one exception is entirely yours to make: tapping for directions hands the destination to your maps application, which is governed by that company's privacy policy, not this one. Nothing about your progress, your photos or your account goes with it, because there is no account.

Children

Summit is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this app ever does start collecting something, for example if accounts and syncing are added later, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be stated plainly here rather than buried.
